# MeetingVA AI Business Plan

## Mission

Help people turn meetings into reliable memory, decisions, and follow-through.

## Market Position

MeetingVA AI should position as a secure, reviewable meeting intelligence workspace rather than only a live call bot. The wedge is browser-based capture plus structured post-meeting intelligence.

## Target Market

Initial customers:

- Solo consultants and advisors.
- Founders and operators.
- Small professional services teams.
- Product and customer-facing teams that need searchable meeting records.

Expansion customers:

- Agencies.
- Customer success teams.
- Sales teams.
- Internal operations teams.
- Regulated teams after stronger compliance controls.

## Core Value Proposition

- Capture meeting audio without a complicated setup.
- Store meeting records securely.
- Generate transcripts and summaries.
- Extract decisions, action items, and questions.
- Review speakers and source transcript segments.
- Build a searchable memory layer over time.

## Revenue Model

Use a freemium SaaS model with paid plans based on processing volume, collaboration, retention, exports, and administration.

Initial monetization should prioritize:

- Pro subscriptions for individual power users.
- Team plans for small teams.
- Enterprise plans for security, retention, support, and custom workflow needs.

## Product Strategy

MVP:

- Reliable meeting capture, transcription, and AI analysis.
- Clear setup and deployment.
- Strong operational docs.

v1.1:

- Transcript editing.
- Search.
- PDF and DOCX export.
- Processing retries and stronger review workflows.
- Product analytics.

v2.0:

- Team workspaces.
- Calendar integration.
- AI chat over meetings.
- Billing.
- Observability and compliance controls.

## Go-To-Market Strategy

Use a founder-led launch:

- Publish a simple demo workflow.
- Recruit 10 to 20 design partners.
- Focus on consultants, founders, and small teams.
- Measure first-recording activation, transcription success, and repeat use.
- Convert high-frequency users into Pro or Team plans.

## Risks

- AI processing cost may exceed early revenue if limits are not enforced.
- Transcription quality and speaker accuracy may vary by audio quality.
- Existing competitors have brand recognition and meeting platform integrations.
- Privacy expectations are high for meeting data.

## Mitigations

- Add processing quotas and cost monitoring before broad launch.
- Keep short recordings and controlled beta usage during early testing.
- Differentiate on reviewability, secure storage, and post-meeting workflows.
- Keep service role keys backend-only and maintain private storage defaults.
