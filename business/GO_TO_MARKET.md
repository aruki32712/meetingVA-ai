# MeetingVA AI Go-To-Market Plan

## Launch Objective

Validate that target users will repeatedly record meetings, generate transcripts, review AI outputs, and rely on MeetingVA AI as a post-meeting memory system.

## Initial Audience

- Consultants who need client meeting records.
- Founders who need lightweight operating memory.
- Product managers who need decision and action-item capture.
- Customer success and sales operators who need searchable customer calls.

## Positioning

MeetingVA AI is the meeting memory workspace for people who need more than scattered notes. It captures source audio, creates transcripts, extracts decisions and action items, and keeps meeting history reviewable.

## Launch Phases

### Private Alpha

- Use internal test accounts.
- Validate deployment and processing reliability.
- Run short recordings only.
- Fix blocker-level workflow issues.

### Design Partner Beta

- Recruit 10 to 20 users from the target personas.
- Onboard each user personally.
- Ask each user to process at least three real meetings.
- Track activation, processing success, output usefulness, and repeat use.

### Public MVP Launch

- Publish demo video or walkthrough.
- Launch a simple landing page.
- Offer Free and Pro waitlist or checkout.
- Use founder-led outreach and product communities.

## Channels

- Founder network.
- LinkedIn founder/operator posts.
- Consultant and agency communities.
- Product management communities.
- Short demo clips.
- Customer interview follow-ups.

## Messaging Tests

- "Turn meetings into searchable memory."
- "Capture decisions and action items without rewriting notes."
- "A secure post-meeting AI workspace."
- "Meeting transcripts, summaries, and follow-up in one place."

## Launch Metrics

- Signup conversion.
- First meeting recorded.
- First transcript generated.
- First analysis generated.
- Time to useful output.
- Repeat meeting processed within seven days.
- Qualitative usefulness rating.
