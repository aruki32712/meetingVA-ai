# MeetingVA AI KPIs

## North Star

Useful meetings processed per active user per month.

A useful processed meeting means the user recorded or uploaded a meeting, generated a transcript, and generated or reviewed AI meeting intelligence.

## Activation Metrics

- Signup to first meeting recorded.
- First meeting recorded to first transcript generated.
- First transcript generated to first analysis generated.
- Time from signup to first useful processed meeting.
- Percentage of new users with one useful meeting in 24 hours.

## Product Quality Metrics

- Transcription job success rate.
- Analysis job success rate.
- Average processing time.
- Speaker edit rate.
- Transcript correction rate when editor exists.
- Export usage when exports exist.

## Retention Metrics

- Weekly active users.
- Meetings processed per active user.
- Repeat meeting within seven days.
- Four-week retention.
- Team workspace active seats when Team exists.

## Business Metrics

- Free to Pro conversion.
- Pro churn.
- Team conversion.
- Average revenue per account.
- Gross margin after AI and storage costs.
- Cost per processed meeting.

## Support And Reliability Metrics

- Failed job count.
- Deployment incidents.
- Mean time to recovery.
- Support tickets per active account.
- Customer-reported output usefulness.

## KPI Review Cadence

- Daily during private alpha: processing success, errors, activation.
- Weekly during beta: activation, retention, qualitative feedback.
- Monthly after launch: revenue, churn, margin, cohort retention.
