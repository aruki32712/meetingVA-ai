# MeetingVA AI Pricing

This is a proposed pricing model. Validate willingness to pay before publishing.

## Free

For testing the product and light personal use.

- Limited monthly recording/transcription minutes.
- Meeting recording and upload.
- Transcript generation.
- Basic summary.
- Limited retention.
- Community or self-serve support.

Primary goal: drive activation and product feedback.

## Pro

For individual professionals who rely on meeting memory.

Suggested price: $19 to $29 per user per month.

- Higher monthly transcription minutes.
- AI summaries, action items, decisions, and questions.
- Speaker review.
- Search when available.
- PDF/DOCX export when available.
- Longer retention.

Primary goal: monetize consultants, founders, and operators.

## Team

For small teams that need shared meeting history.

Suggested price: $12 to $20 per seat per month plus usage limits.

- Shared workspace.
- Team meeting library.
- Role-based access when available.
- Shared search and exports.
- Admin controls.
- Priority support.

Primary goal: expand from individual use to collaborative meeting memory.

## Enterprise

For larger organizations with security, compliance, support, or integration requirements.

Suggested price: custom annual contract.

- Custom retention and data controls.
- SSO when available.
- Audit and compliance support.
- Dedicated support.
- Custom integrations.
- Volume processing terms.

Primary goal: monetize high-value teams without forcing enterprise complexity into the MVP.

## Pricing Principles

- Tie limits to expensive resources: transcription minutes, storage, analysis, retention, and seats.
- Keep Free useful but bounded.
- Make Pro clearly better for repeat individual use.
- Make Team valuable through collaboration, not just bigger quotas.
- Add cost monitoring before expanding public usage.
