# Walter Labs AI Business Docs

Start with `EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY.md`, then use the remaining files based on the decision being made.

## Files

- `EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY.md`: company, product, problem, solution, and current stage.
- `BUSINESS_PLAN.md`: strategy, market, revenue model, risks, and mitigations.
- `PRICING.md`: proposed Free, Pro, Team, and Enterprise packaging.
- `GO_TO_MARKET.md`: launch phases, channels, messaging, and launch metrics.
- `COMPETITOR_ANALYSIS.md`: competitor categories and differentiation.
- `CUSTOMER_PERSONAS.md`: ideal customer profiles and success moments.
- `KPIS.md`: north star, activation, quality, retention, business, and reliability metrics.
- `FOUNDER_DASHBOARD.md`: CEO dashboard for priorities, metrics, risks, and decisions.

## Update Rule

When product scope, pricing, customer focus, or launch strategy changes, update the relevant business file and reflect the current state in `../docs/PROJECT_STATUS.md`.
