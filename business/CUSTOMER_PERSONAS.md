# MeetingVA AI Customer Personas

## Solo Consultant

Needs:

- Reliable client meeting records.
- Summaries and action items.
- Exportable notes.
- Low setup burden.

Buying trigger:

- Too much time spent rewriting notes after client calls.

Success moment:

- A client meeting becomes a clean transcript, summary, and action list within minutes.

## Founder Or Operator

Needs:

- Memory across investor, hiring, product, and customer meetings.
- Decisions and open questions.
- Searchable context.

Buying trigger:

- Important commitments are being lost across fast-moving meetings.

Success moment:

- The founder can recover a decision and supporting transcript from a previous meeting.

## Product Manager

Needs:

- Customer and stakeholder call records.
- Decisions and follow-up tracking.
- Search across meeting history.

Buying trigger:

- Product decisions are spread across docs, chats, and memory.

Success moment:

- A meeting produces a brief, decisions, and open questions ready for follow-up.

## Customer Success Or Sales Operator

Needs:

- Customer call notes.
- Account-specific history.
- Follow-up items.
- Future CRM integration.

Buying trigger:

- Manual notes are slowing down customer follow-up.

Success moment:

- The operator extracts action items and next steps immediately after a call.

## Small Team Lead

Needs:

- Shared meeting library.
- Team-level accountability.
- Search and exports.

Buying trigger:

- Meetings are creating commitments that are hard to track across the team.

Success moment:

- The team can find decisions and owners from past meetings without asking around.
