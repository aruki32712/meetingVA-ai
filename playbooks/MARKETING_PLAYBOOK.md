# Marketing Playbook

## Purpose

Use this playbook to plan and execute founder-led marketing for MeetingVA AI.

## Core Message

MeetingVA AI turns meetings into searchable memory, decisions, and follow-up.

## Launch Assets

- Short product demo.
- Screenshot or walkthrough of record, transcript, summary, and action items.
- Landing page or README-based product explainer.
- Founder post announcing design partner access.
- Onboarding email for new beta users.

## Content Angles

- Meetings create work; MeetingVA AI captures what matters.
- Decisions should not disappear into chat history.
- Consultants need client meeting memory without manual note cleanup.
- Founders need an operating memory across fast-moving conversations.

## Weekly Marketing Loop

1. Publish one product update.
2. Contact five target users.
3. Book two user conversations.
4. Watch one user process a meeting.
5. Record objections and language in customer notes.
6. Feed insights into product roadmap and pricing.
